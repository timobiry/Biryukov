About Me

I am an Undergraduate Research Assistant at Virginia Tech's Power Electronics Materials, Modules, and Manufacturing Laboratory pursuing a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering. My work focuses on high-heat-flux thermal management, two-phase and jet-impingement cooling, and wide-bandgap Silicon Carbide (SiC) power module packaging, combining ANSYS Fluent CFD simulations, CAD design, and MATLAB/Python analysis with experimental validation.
