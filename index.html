About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering senior at Virginia Tech and an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the Power Electronics Materials, Modules, and Manufacturing Laboratory. My research focuses on high-heat-flux thermal management and wide-bandgap Silicon Carbide (SiC) power module packaging, with an emphasis on two-phase and jet-impingement cooling. I enjoy working across the full development cycle—taking thermal solutions from initial CAD design and Ansys Fluent CFD modeling through experimental validation and MATLAB/Python data analysis.
