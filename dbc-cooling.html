Water Jet-Impingement Cooling

CFD Simulation & Experimental Validation

Overview

Designed and experimentally validated a water jet-impingement cooling system for an Office of Naval Research (ONR) project, aiming to maximize thermal dissipation in concentrated areas.


Methodology

  • Modeled cooling manifold dimensions and jet nozzle arrays to optimize impingement zones.
  • Executed CFD simulations to predict flow behavior and thermal distribution.
  • Validated simulated findings with experimental bench testing, accurately matching target thermal resistance values.
